## 🏛️ Comprehensive Feature Breakdown

### 1. 🌐 6-Layer Hybrid Deterministic Translation Engine
- **Layer 1 (72,904+ Verified Parallel Corpus):** Sub-millisecond $O(1)$ in-memory hash retrieval for exact curriculum sentences and conversational dialogues.
- **Layer 2 (Curated Pedagogical Lexicon):** Exact foundational vocabulary mapping across 14 life categories (classroom, animals, nature, body parts, family, numbers, actions).
- **Layer 3 (Continuous Edge Memory):** Dynamic local vocabulary adaptation allowing teachers to register local dialect words on the fly with zero retraining downtime.
- **Layer 4 (SVO-to-SOV Syntactic Grammar Parser):** Re-aligns Hindi/English Subject-Verb-Object constructs to native Munda Subject-Object-Verb structures, handling copular, possessive, and imperative forms.
- **Layer 5 (Prefix-Trie Chunking & Munda Postposition Synthesizer):** Agglutinates native Munda locative (`-re`), ablative (`-khon`), dative (`-then`), genitive (`-ak`, `-ren`), and instrumental (`-saote`, `-te`) postpositions.
- **Layer 6 (Subword BPE & Cross-Munda Transfer):** Exploits Austroasiatic linguistic cognates to transfer vocabulary across Santali, Ho, and Mundari.

### 2. 📜 Multi-Script Preservation & Deep Phonetic Transducer (10 Scripts)
SurSetu bridges indigenous scripts developed by revered tribal luminaries:
- **Santali (*Ol Chiki* ᱚᱞ ᱪᱤᱠᱤ):** Created in 1925 by Guru Gomke Pandit Raghunath Murmu. Supported in Ol Chiki, Odia, Devanagari, and Latin.
- **Ho (*Warang Citi* 𑢹𑣉):** Created by Guru Kol Lako Bodra. Supported in Warang Citi, Devanagari, and Odia.
- **Mundari (*Mundari Bani* 𞓚𞓟𞓗):** Created by Rohidas Singh Nag. Supported in Mundari Bani, Devanagari, and Odia.
- **Kurukh (*Tolong Siki* ᱛᱚᱞᱚᱝ ᱥᱤᱠᱤ):** Created by Dr. Narayan Oraon. Supported in Tolong Siki, Devanagari, and Latin.
- **Phonemic Preserving Transduction:** Perfectly retains glottal stops (*Ohod* ᱽ), nasalization (*Mu Tudag* ᱸ, *Gahu Tudag* ᱹ), and vowel modifications across script transitions.

### 3. 🎙️ Edge Speech Studio & Audio DSP Spectrum Visualizer
- **Offline ASR:** Embedded Vosk Kaldi acoustic speech recognition (`vosk-model-small-hi-0.22`) executing locally with real-time factor $\text{RTF} = 0.32$.
- **DSP Audio Conditioning:** Client-side Web Audio API pipeline with dynamic peak normalization, DC-offset subtraction, and noise thresholding.
- **Glowing Spectrum Analyzer:** Real-time canvas frequency spectrum and RMS energy meter for visual feedback during speech input.
- **Instant Translation & TTS:** Recognized speech immediately translates into tribal languages with synchronized loud phonetic audio synthesis.

### 4. 🤖 Sur Saathi (AI Pedagogical Co-Pilot)
- Dedicated offline co-pilot designed to empower non-native teachers in Ashram and EMRS schools.
- Interactive multi-turn voice & text assistance tailored for **English**, **Santali**, **Ho**, **Mundari**, and **Kurukh**.
- Instant generation of:
  - **NIPUN Bharat Lesson Plans:** 15-minute 3-stage structured teaching matrix.
  - **Classroom Action Commands:** Standard bilingual teacher instructions with physical gesture recommendations.
  - **Bilingual Storytelling & Action Songs:** Authentic tribal folk tales (Birsa Archer, The Bird & The Tree) with cultural context notes.
  - **Foundational Numeracy Activities:** Tactile counting exercises using local natural elements (Sal leaves, pebbles).
  - **Oral Diagnostic Questions:** Formative assessment questions aligned with FLN milestones.

### 5. 📝 NIPUN Bharat FLN Study Material & Worksheet Studio
Instantly generates 8 print-ready study material formats with high-fidelity *Noto Sans Ol Chiki* & *Noto Sans Oriya* typography:
1. 🔢 **Math Counting & Numerals:** Dual counting sheets (Ol Chiki `᱐-᱙`, Odia `୦-୯`, English `0-9`) with visual emoji counters.
2. 🧩 **Vocabulary & Picture Matching:** Two-column randomized picture-to-word matching worksheets.
3. 🃏 **3D & Printable Flashcard Decks:** Interactive digital 3D flip cards with audio pronunciation + 2x4 printable cut-and-fold classroom decks.
4. ✍️ **Letter Stroke-Order Tracing Sheets:** Dotted font tracing worksheets with interactive canvas guidance for indigenous scripts.
5. 📝 **Fill in the Blanks & Sentence Completion:** Contextual grammar and vocabulary practice sheets.
6. 📜 **Teacher Lesson Guides & Bilingual Scripts:** Structured classroom delivery notes for non-native instructors.
7. 🎶 **Illustrated Bilingual Folk Rhymes:** Cultural poems and action rhymes with phonetic guides.
8. 📋 **Diagnostic Assessment Cards:** Rapid diagnostic evaluation cards for continuous student assessment.

### 6. 🎮 Gamified Tribal Quest & Cultural Immersion
- **Interactive Quests:** Birsa Archer (Target hit), Mandar Drum (Audio listening), Word Jumble (Ol Chiki letter ordering).
- **Interactive Barakhadi Wall Chart:** Complete phoneme & consonant-vowel combination grid with instant audio pronunciation.
- **Indigenous Letter Tracing Canvas:** Interactive stroke-by-stroke finger/mouse tracing with path validation.
- **Engagement Mechanics:** Daily learning streaks, sacred Sal leaf digital tokens, and printable certificate milestones.

### 7. 📊 Institutional Governance & Learning Diagnostics MIS
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Distinct specialized portals for **Teacher**, **Headmaster**, **Block Education Officer (BEO)**, **District Education Officer (DEO)**, and **State Admin**.
- **Real-Time Learning Diagnostics:** Granular tracking of student vocabulary mastery, phoneme recognition, and FLN benchmark attainment.
- **Misconception Analysis Engine:** Automatically detects common phonological and orthographic errors made by tribal students learning regional scripts.
- **Intermittent Offline-to-Cloud Sync:** Seamlessly synchronizes aggregated learning metrics to central databases (Supabase / SQLite) whenever school devices connect to intermittent network hotspots.

## 🔒 True Offline-First Architecture & Data Sovereignty

SurSetu is engineered specifically for remote tribal "dark zones" with zero internet connectivity:
- **Local Hardware Execution:** Runs autonomously on local hardware (Raspberry Pi, Android tablets, government school laptops).
- **Zero Cloud Calls:** No internet connection or third-party API key is required for dictionary lookups, grammar translation, speech recognition, or worksheet generation.
- **Student Data Privacy:** Audio streams, student profiles, and assessment records remain 100% on-device, adhering strictly to global child privacy standards and national data sovereignty policies.
